Source: DHLGCD Fact sheet FS17 "Repairs and maintenance" (version 10/25), https://dhlgcd.nt.gov.au/media/documents/fact-sheets/repairs-and-maintenance-fs17.pdf (read 6 Oct 2026 via browser + pdf.js)

Report examples: blocked toilets or drains; leaking sewerage/sewage coming up pipes; leaking water from taps/pipes; exposed electrical wires; gas leaks; dripping taps/taps tight to turn; broken locks or door handles; stove elements not working; smoke alarms not working; broken doors; fans not working properly; power points not working; a dangerous electrical fault; flooding or serious flood damage; a serious roof leak; serious storm, fire or impact damage.

Response times ("A contractor will contact you based on the following"):
- Immediate maintenance - response time within 4 hours.
- Urgent maintenance - within 2 business days. Remote housing response time is within 5 business days.
- Routine maintenance - within ten (10) business days. Remote housing response time is within 25 business days.
"In remote areas, Remote Housing Maintenance Officers will step in to assess and make safe if a contractor isn't available."
Remote Housing hotline / Maintenance Call Centre 1800 104 076. Regional offices: Greater Darwin, Palmerston, Big Rivers, Barkly, Arnhem, Top End, Central Australia.
Tenant may pay for intentional or negligent damage; late reporting can make tenant responsible for additional damage.
